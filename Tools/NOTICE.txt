DragConvert bundles the following command-line image tools:

- cwebp 1.6.0 (libwebp) — BSD-3-Clause
  Source: https://chromium.googlesource.com/webm/libwebp
  Universal binary: arm64 slice from the Homebrew bottle, patched to load
  its libraries from the same folder with @loader_path; x86_64 slice built
  statically from libwebp 1.6.0 + libpng 1.6.59.

- libpng 1.6.59 — libpng License
  Source: http://www.libpng.org/pub/png/libpng.html

- libjpeg-turbo 3.2.1 — BSD-style / IJG licenses
  Source: https://github.com/libjpeg-turbo/libjpeg-turbo

The corresponding license texts are in LICENSES/.
